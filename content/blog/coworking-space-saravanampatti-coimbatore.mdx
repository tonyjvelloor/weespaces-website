## 5-Step Checklist for Choosing the Best Workspace in Saravanampatti

Before finalizing your workspace agreement, evaluate your shortlisted providers against these five practical criteria:

1. **Verify Commute & Road Accessibility:** Is the building directly accessible from Sathy Road? Are there convenient public transit links, bus stops, and dedicated two-wheeler and four-wheeler parking facilities for your team and visitors?
2. **Test Internet Speed & Failover:** Don't just rely on marketing brochures. Visit the space during peak hours (around 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM), run a speed and latency test, and confirm whether they have dual active fiber-optic connections from distinct Internet Service Providers.
3. **Inspect Power Backup Readiness:** Confirm that the facility utilizes online UPS systems that prevent computers from rebooting during power switchovers, backed by industrial diesel generators capable of sustaining continuous air conditioning.
4. **Scrutinize Hidden Charges:** Ensure pricing quotes are transparent. Inquire whether high-speed internet, air conditioning, printer usage, housekeeping, and meeting room access are included in your monthly membership fee or billed as surprise line items.
5. **Evaluate the Professional Atmosphere:** Spend a trial day working in the community. Observe noise levels, desk spacing, natural lighting, and the responsiveness of on-site community managers.
