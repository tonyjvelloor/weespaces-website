## What is a Managed Office Space?

A Managed Office (also known as a Build-to-Suit enterprise suite) is a fully customized, private office environment built exclusively for a single tenant, while the workspace operator handles 100% of property procurement, interior fit-outs, facility maintenance, IT infrastructure, and daily housekeeping under a single monthly bill.

Unlike standard coworking spaces where amenities are shared across multiple companies, a managed office provides:
- **Dedicated Private Entrance & Access Control:** Biometric or RFID access for your staff only.
- **Custom Branded Interiors:** Layout tailored to your operational workflows (private executive cabins, agile pod desks, dedicated server rooms, and branded reception walls).
- **Dedicated IT Infrastructure:** Custom VLAN configurations, dedicated bandwidth pipes, dual-ISP failover, and isolated firewall hardware.
- **Private Internal Meeting Rooms & Breakout Lounges:** Exclusive boardrooms inside your suite so confidential discussions remain private.
