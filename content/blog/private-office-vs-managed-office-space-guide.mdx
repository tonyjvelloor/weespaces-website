## Quick Definitions: What is the Difference?

Before diving into granular comparisons, let us establish the clear distinction:

### 1. Private Office Cabin
A **Private Office** is an enclosed, lockable room located within a larger coworking facility. The internal space (desks, ergonomic chairs, and whiteboards) is reserved exclusively for your team, while core infrastructure—such as the reception desk, cafeteria/pantry, meeting rooms, phone booths, and restrooms—is shared with other member companies.
- **Typical Team Size:** 2 to 20 Seats.
- **Commitment Horizon:** Highly flexible (1 to 12 Months).
- **Setup Speed:** Instant (Plug-and-play within 24 hours).

### 2. Managed Office (Build-to-Suit Enterprise Suite)
A **Managed Office** is a fully customized, private office floor or dedicated suite built from the ground up to your exact architectural, technical, and branding specifications. The operator (such as WeeSpaces) procures the commercial real estate, designs the layout, funds the capital expenditure (CapEx), manages daily operations, and delivers the entire floor under a single consolidated operational invoice.
- **Typical Team Size:** 20 to 150+ Seats.
- **Commitment Horizon:** 12 to 36 Months.
- **Setup Speed:** Turnkey handover in 30 to 45 Days.

## IT & Security Comparison: Critical for Software & GCC Teams

For technology engineering centers, fintech startups, and Global Capability Centers (GCCs), IT governance often dictates workspace selection:

### Private Office Cabin Security:
In a high-grade private cabin at WeeSpaces, your team connects to enterprise-grade internet equipped with **virtual LAN (VLAN) isolation**. This ensures devices on your subnetwork cannot be discovered by other companies in the building. However, server hardware and internet lines enter through the shared facility core.

### Managed Office Enterprise Security:
In a dedicated managed suite, you retain full architectural control of your technology perimeter:
- **Private Server Closets:** Dedicated air-conditioned server racks with biometric dual-factor entry.
- **Dual Redundant Leased Lines:** Dedicated 1:1 symmetrical bandwidth lines from Tier-1 ISPs with automatic BGP failover.
- **SOC2 & ISO Compliance:** Physical separation, clean-desk policy compliance, and dedicated surveillance camera streams routed directly to your global Security Operations Center (SOC).
