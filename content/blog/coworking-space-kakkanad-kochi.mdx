## Key Office Hubs Around Kakkanad & Infopark

When looking for office space in Kakkanad, understanding the micro-location landscape is vital for client accessibility and employee convenience:

### 1. Infopark Phase 1 & Phase 2
Infopark is Kerala’s flagship government IT hub. Phase 1 houses major corporate campuses (including TCS, Wipro, and Cognizant), while Phase 2 hosts modern private IT towers. Flexible coworking spaces operating along the Infopark Expressway provide boutique agencies and growing IT teams direct proximity to enterprise clients without corporate lease hurdles.

### 2. SmartCity Kochi
SmartCity is a joint venture between the Government of Kerala and TECOM Group (Dubai). Featuring international architectural standards, LEED-certified infrastructure, and special economic zone (SEZ) benefits, SmartCity is ideal for global IT exports. Coworking spaces near SmartCity allow boutique technology partners to operate with world-class prestige.

### 3. Civil Station & Rajagiri Corridor
Located along the main Kakkanad-Edappally road, the Civil Station area connects civic administration with educational hubs like Rajagiri. This corridor is popular among fintechs, law firms, and consulting startups seeking high footfall and excellent public transport links.
