## Critical Amenities to Evaluate Before Signing a Workspace Contract

Not all shared offices are created equal. When evaluating coworking spaces in Kochi, verify these five essentials:

1. **Automatic Diesel Generator (DG) Power Backup:** Kerala’s monsoon season frequently causes grid power drops. Inadequate battery inverters fail under heavy AC loads. Ensure your provider operates a dedicated DG system with automatic transfer switching.
2. **Dual-ISP Redundant Fiber Lines:** Software development and Zoom/Teams calls demand 99.9% uptime. Verify that your workspace operates dual redundant leased lines from different Tier-1 providers.
3. **Ergonomic Furniture:** Look for high-back breathable mesh chairs with lumbar adjustment and full-sized 4-foot desks.
4. **Sound-Insulated Acoustic Phone Booths:** Taking private client calls or investor pitches in an open hall creates acoustic distractions. High-grade spaces provide dedicated phone booths.
5. **Meeting Room & Boardroom Availability:** Ensure your monthly membership includes booking credits for client presentations with 4K screens.
