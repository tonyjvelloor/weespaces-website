## 1. Kakkanad & Infopark Corridor (Tech & Export Leader)

## 2. Ravipuram & MG Road (Commercial Core)

For traditional businesses, law firms, chartered accountants, and retail distributors, MG Road and Ravipuram represent the central CBD (Central Business District) of Ernakulam.

* **Accessibility:** Located right on the Metro corridor with quick access to North/South railway stations.
* **Credibility:** A central Ernakulam address adds weight on invoice footers, bank account applications, and legal agreements.
* **Best For:** Financial services, trading firms, consulting agencies, and legal practices.
