### Stage 1: Digital Signature Certificates (DSC)
All directors and shareholders must possess a valid Class-3 Digital Signature Certificate to electronically sign MCA application forms.

Propose two unique business names adhering to the Companies Act rules. The MCA usually approves or suggests modifications within 2 to 3 working days.
