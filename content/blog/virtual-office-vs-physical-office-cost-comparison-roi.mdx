## The Hidden Sunk Costs of Traditional Commercial Leases

When founders sign a standard commercial rental agreement, base rent accounts for only **55% to 65%** of the true financial outlay. The remaining 35% to 45% consists of "invisible" operational drains:

```
True Breakdown of Traditional Office Spend:
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Base Commercial Rent: 58%                              │
│ Interior Fit-Out Amortization: 15%                     │
│ Trapped Capital in Landlord Deposit: 10%               │
│ Electricity, Air Conditioning & DG Fuel: 9%            │
│ Janitorial, Facility Staff & Cleaning: 5%              │
│ Redundant Dual ISPs & Networking: 3%                   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Trapped Working Capital:** Security deposits of ₹3 Lakhs to ₹6 Lakhs sit in landlords' personal bank accounts without generating interest or investment return.
2. **Fit-Out Write-Offs:** When your lease ends, 100% of the money spent on wall partitions, false ceilings, electrical cabling, and paint is non-recoverable.
3. **Management Distraction:** Founders and operations leads spend hours coordinating AC technicians, waste disposal, cleaning staff, and broadband complaints instead of building product and acquiring customers.
