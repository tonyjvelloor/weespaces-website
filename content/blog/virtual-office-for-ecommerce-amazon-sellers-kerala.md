If you are an e-commerce seller expanding your business in India, you are required by law to register for GST in every state where you store goods. For sellers expanding into Kerala using Amazon FBA or Flipkart Fulfillment, this means you need a local Kerala address to register as an **APOB (Additional Place of Business)** or **PPOB (Principal Place of Business)**.

Renting a physical office just to meet this requirement is an unnecessary drain on your capital. The most efficient, legal, and cost-effective solution is a [Virtual Office](/virtual-office).

## Why E-Commerce Sellers Need a Kerala Address

When you ship inventory to an Amazon FBA warehouse or Flipkart fulfillment center in Kerala, that warehouse acts as an Additional Place of Business (APOB) under GST law. 

To add a Kerala warehouse to your GST certificate, you must first have a Principal Place of Business (PPOB) in the state. If you are a seller based in Delhi, Mumbai, or Bangalore, you cannot use your home state address as the PPOB for a Kerala GST registration. You need a local address.

## How a Virtual Office Solves the Problem

A Virtual Office provides e-commerce sellers with a premium, legally compliant commercial address in Kerala (such as Kochi, Trivandrum, or Calicut) without the expense of renting physical space.

## Documents Required for PPOB Registration

When applying for GST in Kerala using a Virtual Office, you will need to upload:
- A formally executed **Rental Agreement** between you and the workspace operator.
- A **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** from the property owner.
- The latest **Utility Bill** for the premises.
