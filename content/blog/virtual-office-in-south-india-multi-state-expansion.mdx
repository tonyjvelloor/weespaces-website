## Why South India Expansion Requires Multi-State GST

Under the Indian GST framework:
* To sell goods via Amazon FBA or Flipkart warehouses in a new state, you must hold an **Additional Place of Business (APoB)** registration in that specific state.
* To bill regional clients locally and claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) on intra-state supply, you require a state-specific GSTIN under your **Principal Place of Business (PPoB)**.

Renting physical offices in Kochi, Trivandrum, Calicut, and Coimbatore just to meet tax compliance can cost upwards of ₹5,000,000 annually in rent and deposits. A multi-state virtual office network solves this problem instantly.
