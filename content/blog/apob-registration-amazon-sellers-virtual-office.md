If you are an e-commerce seller on Amazon India, Flipkart, or Shopify, you already know the golden rule of scaling: **you need your inventory stored as close to your customers as possible.**

To access Amazon's Prime delivery speeds across South India, you must utilize their Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) warehouses. However, Indian GST law throws a major curveball at sellers: you cannot store your goods in a warehouse located in a different state unless you have a registered GST number in that state. And to get that GST number, you need an **Additional Place of Business (APoB)**.

Here is exactly how smart sellers are bypassing expensive commercial leases and using a [Virtual Office for Amazon Sellers](/virtual-office/amazon-sellers) to secure their APoB instantly.

## The APoB Problem Explained

Imagine you are a seller based in Delhi. You want to store your products in an Amazon FBA warehouse in Tamil Nadu to offer next-day delivery to customers in Chennai, Coimbatore, and Kochi.

1. Amazon requires you to add their warehouse address as your APoB on your GST certificate.
2. The GST department will not allow you to register an APoB in Tamil Nadu unless you already have a **Principal Place of Business (PPoB)** registered in Tamil Nadu.
3. To get a PPoB in Tamil Nadu, you need a local physical address, complete with a Rental Agreement, NOC, and Utility Bill.

Are you going to rent a 1,000 sq. ft. commercial office in Coimbatore for ₹50,000 a month just to satisfy this paperwork requirement? **Absolutely not.**
