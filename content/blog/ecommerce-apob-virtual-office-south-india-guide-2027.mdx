## 1. The Legal Requirement: PPoB vs. APoB Explained

Understanding the statutory hierarchy under the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 is essential:

```
Step 1: Obtain a State-Specific Virtual Office (WeeSpaces Hub)
   └─ Registered as your Principal Place of Business (PPoB) in Kerala or Tamil Nadu
        │
Step 2: Add Marketplace Fulfillment Centers as APoB
   ├─ Amazon FBA Warehouse (e.g., Cochin / Coimbatore FC)
   ├─ Flipkart Fulfillment Center (FBF)
   └─ Quick Commerce Mother Dark Stores
        │
Result: 100% Tax Compliant Local Stocking + Next-Day Prime Badges
```

- **Principal Place of Business (PPoB):** The primary commercial address where your company maintains books of accounts and legal correspondence within the state. You cannot obtain a state-specific GSTIN without a valid PPoB.
- **Additional Place of Business (APoB):** Any secondary premises—such as a third-party logistics (3PL) warehouse, Amazon FC, or cold storage depot—where your inventory is physically stocked. An APoB cannot exist without an active PPoB in the same state.
