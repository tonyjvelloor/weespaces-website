## 2. Invoicing Domestic & International Clients: How It Works

Software founders frequently ask: *"How do we handle client billing with a virtual office address?"*

### A. Export Invoicing (USA, Europe, Middle East, Southeast Asia)
Under Indian GST law, software development, web design, and SaaS subscriptions provided to overseas clients qualify as **Export of Services** (Zero-Rated Supplies under Section 16 of the IGST Act, 2017) provided:
- The supplier is located in India.
- The recipient is located outside India.
- Payment is received in convertible foreign exchange (or INR via authorized Vostro accounts / PayPal / Stripe / Wise).
- By linking your WeeSpaces virtual office GSTIN to an annual **Letter of Undertaking (LUT)**, you invoice international clients without charging 18% IGST or undergoing tedious refund filing cycles.

### B. Domestic Client Invoicing
For clients within India, your invoices carry your state-specific GSTIN (e.g., **State Code 32 for Kerala** or **State Code 33 for Tamil Nadu**), allowing your clients to claim 100% Input Tax Credit (ITC).
