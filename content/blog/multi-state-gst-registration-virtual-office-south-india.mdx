## Understanding PPoB vs. APoB Under GST Law

When expanding into Kerala or Tamil Nadu, your company must determine its registration framework:

### 1. Principal Place of Business (PPoB)
If your company does not currently have any active GST registration in Kerala or Tamil Nadu, you must establish a **Principal Place of Business** in that state to acquire a state-specific GSTIN (starting with **State Code 32** for Kerala and **State Code 33** for Tamil Nadu). 
- WeeSpaces provides the registered commercial lease agreement, property tax receipts, electricity bill, and owner No Objection Certificate (NOC) required for instant portal filing.

### 2. Additional Place of Business (APoB)
If you already possess a state GSTIN but wish to declare an operational hub, warehouse transit point, or regional client liaison desk in another city within the same state (for example, adding Calicut and Trivandrum to an existing Kochi GSTIN), you declare it as an **APoB**.
- E-commerce merchants on Amazon (FBA) and Flipkart rely on APoB virtual offices to unlock regional fulfillment center access and state tax credits.

## Navigating State-Level GST Physical Verification

In recent years, state GST enforcement wings across Kerala and Tamil Nadu have tightened scrutiny on new GST registrations, routinely ordering **in-person physical site visits** by local tax inspectors prior to approving applications.

During a site inspection, the GST officer checks:
1. Whether the commercial building exists and is legitimate Grade-A commercial real estate.
2. Whether your company name and GST details are displayed on the external signage board.
3. Whether an authorized representative or community manager is physically on site to present identity records and the active lease register.

### 🔴 The Fatal Pitfall of Virtual Office Aggregators
Many online aggregators act as middleman brokers who do not own or manage real estate. When a GST inspector conducts an unannounced site visit, there is often no reception staff, no display board, and no tenancy record—leading to **immediate rejection under Rule 21** and blacklisting of director PAN cards.
