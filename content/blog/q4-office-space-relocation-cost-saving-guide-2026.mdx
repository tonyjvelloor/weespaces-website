## The November 30 Workspace Audit Checklist

Before committing to another year of rigid commercial rent, conduct this rapid 5-point audit of your current office overhead:

| Operational Audit Item | Traditional Commercial Lease | Managed Office / Flexible Space | Financial Action Needed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Average Seat Utilization** | Often **40% – 60%** due to hybrid work patterns | **100% Dynamic** (Scale desk count up or down monthly) | Right-size seat allocations to match actual team attendance. |
| **Trapped Capital (Deposits)** | 6 to 10 months rent sitting idle in landlord accounts | **Zero or Minimal Deposit** | Unlock trapped capital to fund growth initiatives in 2027. |
| **Hidden Utility & Maintenance Costs** | ₹15–₹25 / sq.ft over base rent (Power, DG fuel, AC, cleaning) | **All-Inclusive in 1 Invoice** | Eliminate billing surprises and facility management salaries. |
| **Fit-Out Depreciation (CapEx)** | Sunk capital into interior furniture, partitions, and cabling | **100% Financed by Operator** | Shift property expenses from CapEx to tax-deductible OpEx. |
| **Notice Period Window** | 60–90 days notice before lock-in renewal | Flexible 30-day notice terms | Review lease terms before **November 30** to avoid auto-renewals. |
