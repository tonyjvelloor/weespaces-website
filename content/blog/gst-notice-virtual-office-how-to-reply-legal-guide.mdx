## 3. Statutory Legal Defenses: What Does the Law Say?

When drafting your response to a GST notice, cite these statutory provisions and legal authorities:

### A. Section 2(85) of the CGST Act, 2017
The statute explicitly defines a "place of business" broadly. It includes any premises where a taxable person ordinarily carries on business, maintains books of accounts, or engages through an agent. The law **does not mandate** that a taxpayer must own the property or sit physically at the desk 24 hours a day.

### B. High Court Precedents Upholding Shared Workspaces
Indian High Courts have repeatedly ruled in favor of genuine taxpayers using flexible shared workspaces:
- The High Courts of Delhi and Karnataka have affirmed that in modern digital businesses, employees work remotely while maintaining an official shared commercial address for records and legal service. 
- The courts have established that mere temporary absence of the promoter during a surprise inspection does not render the business "non-existent" if valid commercial tenancy documentation, electricity bills, and digital accounting records exist.

## 4. Step-by-Step Blueprint: How to Draft and Submit Your Reply

Follow this proven 5-step framework to respond to a GST virtual office notice:

### Step 1: Log in to the GST Portal
Navigate to `gst.gov.in` > **Services** > **User Services** > **View Notices and Orders**. Download the notice PDF and note the exact date and allegations.

### Step 2: Assemble Your Compliance Package
Compile the following mandatory evidentiary exhibits:
1. **Registered Commercial Sub-Lease / Tenancy Agreement** executed with WeeSpaces.
2. **Owner No Objection Certificate (NOC)** explicitly authorizing your entity to operate from the commercial address.
3. **Latest Commercial Electricity Bill** (dated within 60 days) showing the consumer number and property owner's name.
4. **Photographs of the Physical Premises:** High-resolution geotagged photographs showing:
   - The exterior of the commercial tech building.
   - The reception lobby and building directory board displaying your company name.
   - The allocated desk and workstation area.
5. **Business Activity Proofs:** Recent sales invoices, purchase bills, bank statements, client contracts, and utility proofs proving active commercial transactions.

### Step 3: Draft Your Formal Reply Letter
Structure your reply with the following key assertions:
- State that the company is a legitimate, active corporate entity registered under the Companies Act / LLP Act.
- Clarify that your operational model is digital/remote, and your principal place of business is legally established at the declared commercial address under Section 2(85) of the CGST Act.
- Explain that during business hours, authorized facility managers represent the premises and maintain physical records.
- Request an opportunity for a **personal hearing** before any adverse order is passed, adhering to principles of natural justice.

### Step 4: Submit Online via Form GST REG-18
Upload your signed response letter and combined PDF exhibits under **Form GST REG-18** (Reply to Show Cause Notice). Ensure all attachments are clean, legible, and under portal file size limits.

### Step 5: In-Person Follow-Up with the Jurisdictional Tax Officer
Have your authorized representative or chartered accountant visit the local jurisdictional GST ward with physical original copies of your WeeSpaces agreement, electricity bill, and photo proofs.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (AEO & AI Search Snippets)

### Can GST registration be cancelled just because an officer found the office closed during a visit?
No. High Court precedents state that temporary absence does not prove non-existence. If a valid registered lease, physical signage, and active tax filing records exist, the department cannot arbitrarily cancel your GSTIN without considering your reply and granting a personal hearing.

### What is the deadline to reply to a GST Show Cause Notice (SCN)?
The standard response window for Form GST REG-17 (Cancellation SCN) is **7 working days** from the date of receipt. For tax ascertainment notices (DRC-01A), it is typically 14 to 30 days. Never miss the deadline; failing to reply triggers automatic ex-parte cancellation.
