## What is a Virtual Office in Kerala?

A **Virtual Office in Kerala** provides businesses with a physical commercial address at a recognized IT park or Grade-A commercial complex without requiring daily physical desk usage. 

Under Indian law, the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and the Goods and Services Tax (GST) Department allow businesses to use a virtual office address as their **Principal Place of Business (PPoB)** or **Additional Place of Business (APoB)**, provided specific mandatory documentation is produced.
