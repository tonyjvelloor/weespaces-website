## The Physical Verification Crackdown

Over the past two years, the GST departments in Kerala and Tamil Nadu have cracked down heavily on "fly-by-night" operators using fake addresses. 

When you apply for GST, the local inspector will likely conduct a **Physical Verification**. This means a government official will physically visit your registered address.

If they visit and find a generic residential building, a locked door, or a coworking space that has never heard of your company, your GST application will be rejected, and your PAN card may be flagged.

## E-Commerce Sellers & APOB

If you sell on Amazon or Flipkart and want to use their fulfillment centers (FC) in Kerala or Tamil Nadu, you must register the FC as your Additional Place of Business (APOB). However, to do that, you first need a Principal Place of Business (PPOB) in the state. A Virtual Office serves as the perfect PPOB, allowing you to unlock regional fulfillment and offer next-day delivery to your southern customers.
