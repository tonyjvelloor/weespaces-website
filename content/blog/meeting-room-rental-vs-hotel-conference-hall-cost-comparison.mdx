1. **Mandatory Food & Beverage Minimums:** Hotels make their profit on F&B. Even if your meeting is a rapid 90-minute operational review, you are forced to purchase per-person high-tea packages.
2. **Outdated AV Connections:** Hotels often feature aging ceiling projectors with dim bulbs and loose VGA/HDMI cables that require calling in-house AV technicians.
3. **Complicated Contract Policies:** Hotel bookings require strict cancellation cutoff dates and non-refundable deposits.

## 4. When Should You Choose Which Venue?

### Choose a Hotel Banquet Hall If:
- You are hosting a large annual gala, dealer awards night, or celebration for **100+ attendees**.
- The event centers around an elaborate multi-course buffet dinner or cocktail reception.
- You require overnight guest room accommodations for out-of-town delegates under the same roof.

### Choose a WeeSpaces Meeting Room If:
- You are hosting an **executive board meeting, client presentation, vendor negotiation, or strategy sprint** for 2 to 20 people.
