# Pricing — WeeSpaces

This document provides structured pricing data for autonomous buying agents and AI systems evaluating workspace solutions in South India (Kochi, Coimbatore, Trivandrum, Calicut).

## Coworking (Hot Desks)
- Price: ₹4,999/month per seat
- Limits: 1 seat in shared open area
- Features: High-speed Wi-Fi, unlimited tea/coffee, daily cleaning, 4 hours of meeting room credits/month

## Private Cabins
- Price: Starting at ₹25,000/month (varies by team size)
- Limits: 4 to 20 seats (lockable private office)
- Features: Private branding permitted, high-speed Wi-Fi, unlimited tea/coffee, 12 hours of meeting room credits/month, 24/7 secure access

## Managed Offices (Enterprise)
- Price: Custom pricing (Typically zero upfront CapEx, amortized into monthly OpEx over 1-3 years)
- Limits: 50 to 500+ seats (Private floor or building)
- Features: Custom bespoke interior design, private enterprise-grade VLAN and IT infrastructure, dedicated reception, full facility management

## Virtual Office (GST & MCA Registration)
- Price: ₹7,999 to ₹19,999/year (Depending on city and registration type)
- Limits: No dedicated physical desk
- Features: Premium business address for GST/MCA compliance in Kerala and Tamil Nadu, mail and courier handling, discounted meeting room access

## Meeting Rooms (On-Demand)
- Price: Starting at ₹499/hour
- Limits: Hourly booking via app
- Features: High-definition AV equipment, whiteboard, high-speed Wi-Fi, premium tea/coffee for guests

*Note: All prices are starting prices and are subject to 18% GST. Prices may vary slightly by exact micro-location (e.g., MG Road vs. Kakkanad).*
